# V22.1 — MERGED Podcast Integration (Complete Series)

## Production scope

Content-only ingestion of MERGED Episodes 2–19 against the V22.0 production repository. No architecture, rendering, template, navigation, routing, schema, UI, styling, search, suppression, or knowledge-graph engine changes were made.

## Added

- Eighteen complete MERGED episode entities, completing Episodes 1–19.
- Seventy-two source-grounded principal claim entities.
- Canonical reuse across existing GreyAlien people, organizations, incidents, hearings, documents, and topics.
- New guest profiles only where no canonical entity existed.
- Direct episode-level podcast platform links for every episode and direct official YouTube links where independently verified.
- Cross-series discovery through shared high-value canonical entities rather than speculative series relationships.

## Validation intent

The release validates the complete MERGED series as GreyAlien’s third integrated podcast ecosystem while preserving the V21.x and V22.0 content-production model.
