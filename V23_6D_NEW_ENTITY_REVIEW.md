# V23.6D — New Entity Review

## Result

**One new permanent entity is created in V23.6D: the Sol Foundation policy paper itself.**

The existing Organization and Person entities for The Sol Foundation and Marik von Rennenkampff are reused. No new Topic entities are created.

## The Civilizational Risks of a UAP Technology Arms Race: Nonhuman Technology and Global Security

- **Entity name:** The Civilizational Risks of a UAP Technology Arms Race: Nonhuman Technology and Global Security
- **Entity type:** Publication
- **Entity subtype:** Policy paper
- **Description:** Policy paper by Marik von Rennenkampff examining the global-security risks that could arise if states were competing to exploit advanced technology of alleged nonhuman origin. The analysis treats such an arms race as a policy scenario and does not itself establish that any government possesses nonhuman technology.
- **Why permanent status is warranted:** The paper is substantive source material with enduring research value independent of the moment when it was published. Researchers should remain able to discover it through the Research Library after its news relevance fades.
- **Why metadata alone is insufficient:** Treating the paper only as a transient News Record would lose a durable document-level research destination and prevent future cross-gateway connections to the same publication.
- **Relationships created:** `references_person → marik-von-rennenkampff` (author role); `published_by → sol-foundation`; `references_topic → national-security-secrecy`; `references_topic → non-human-intelligence`; `references_topic → reverse-engineering`; `references_topic → uap-disclosure`.
- **Recommendation:** **KEEP.** This is the intended first demonstration of a current-news item graduating into a permanent Research Library object without duplication.
