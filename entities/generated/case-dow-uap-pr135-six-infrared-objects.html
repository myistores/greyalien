DOW-UAP-PR135 — Six Infrared Objects

PURSUE Release 06 infrared record of six objects over the Middle East, accompanied by a mission-report estimate of approximately 480 mph. A later scientific preprint reports independent 7.1–7.9 Hz brightness fluctuations and proposes nearby birds plus parallax as the best explanation; the interpretation remains provisional.
