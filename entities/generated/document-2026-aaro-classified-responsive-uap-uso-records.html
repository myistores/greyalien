Government Foia Determination / Classified Records Withholding

AARO FOIA Determination on Five UAP/USO Case Records

AARO reported locating records responsive to John Greenewald Jr.'s request concerning five congressionally identified UAP/USO incident labels, then withheld the responsive material in full under classification-related FOIA authority. The determination establishes a records-access outcome, not the authenticity or nature of the underlying incidents.
