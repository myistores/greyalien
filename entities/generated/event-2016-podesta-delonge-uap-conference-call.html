2016 Podesta–DeLonge UAP Conference Call

A 2016 conference call arranged by Tom DeLonge and involving John Podesta, Neil McCasland and Robert Weiss. Podesta confirms that declassification of Department of Defense files and reduction of professional stigma around UAP were discussed, while disputing David Grusch's later characterization of the interaction as an authorized public-acclimatization operation.
