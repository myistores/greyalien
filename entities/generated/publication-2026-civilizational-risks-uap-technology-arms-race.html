The Civilizational Risks of a UAP Technology Arms Race: Nonhuman Technology and Global Security

Policy paper by Marik von Rennenkampff examining the global-security risks that could arise if states were competing to exploit advanced technology of alleged nonhuman origin. The paper treats such an arms race as a consequential policy scenario and argues for international cooperation and control mechanisms; it does not itself establish that any government possesses nonhuman technology.
