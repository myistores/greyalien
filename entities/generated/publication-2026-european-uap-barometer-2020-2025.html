Updating the European UAP Barometer: Reporting Trends and New Data Sources, 2020–2025

Philippe Ailleris's Version 1.0 update aggregates European UAP reporting data for 2020–2025 across national organizations, MUFON, NUFORC and Enigma Labs while emphasizing that report counts measure reporting-system activity rather than the true frequency of anomalous phenomena.
