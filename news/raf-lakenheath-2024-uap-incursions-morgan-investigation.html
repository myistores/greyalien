RAF Lakenheath UAP Investigation: Former Police Officer Questions Official Drone Findings

In the October 21, 2026 episode of Reality Check, journalist Ross Coulthart interviews former British police investigator Mike Morgan and an aviation observer known as Liberty about reported aerial activity near RAF Lakenheath, Mildenhall, Feltwell and Fairford in November 2024.

What the interview establishes

The recording establishes that Morgan and Liberty publicly described their investigations and observations. It does not independently establish the identity, capabilities, origin or operators of the objects.

Freedom of Information and police inquiry

Morgan describes information requests and questions the thoroughness of an official investigation. He also discusses a reported police-helicopter near miss. GreyAlien has not independently obtained or authenticated the underlying FOI correspondence, helicopter report or full investigation file. Those records remain research priorities.

Witness observations

Liberty recounts a silent object he says he observed before the main reported incursions and radio communications he says he heard. These are attributed witness statements, not independently verified sensor findings.

Alternative explanations and limits

Conventional drones, unauthorized surveillance and misidentification remain possible explanations. The interview discusses Russian or Chinese involvement, but no responsible operator is established here. Discussion of the installations' potential nuclear roles should not be read as verification that nuclear weapons were present at each site during the incidents.
