AARO 2023 Radar Detection — Why Track Continuity Matters

DOW-UAP-D165 · AARO radar assessment · 2023 event; public release November 20, 2026

Release inventories describe a radar analysis involving eight detections over an 84-second period. A conditional estimate of approximately 1,650 mph (Mach 2.5 under assumed conditions) has been discussed, but it depends on attributing the separate returns to one moving target.

Critical measurement question

A radar sweep interval of roughly 12 seconds does not by itself demonstrate track continuity. Without the raw radar data, geometry, timing uncertainties, target association method and alternative-track assessment, an extraordinary speed or acceleration cannot be established.

Evidence classification

Documented: A government-released radar assessment identified as DOW-UAP-D165. Conditional: Speed derived by connecting detections. Not demonstrated: One object's continuous trajectory or anomalous propulsion.
