BAASS Ten-Month Report — AAWSAP Contractor Records

DOW-UAP-D172 · BAASS ten-month report · July 2009; public release November 20, 2026

Release inventories identify a 538-page BAASS contractor report prepared during the Defense Intelligence Agency's AAWSAP program. The report describes investigative activities, proposed infrastructure and research work; its statements should be evaluated as contractor reporting rather than independently corroborated findings.

What can be established?

The released record documents the existence of a contractor report and the administrative context of AAWSAP. Descriptions of proposed storage facilities for unusual material do not establish that such a facility was constructed, that materials were obtained, or that any materials were nonhuman in origin.

Historical context

Related Release 08 files include the 2008 AAWSAP statement of work (DOW-UAP-D182), correspondence involving Senator Harry Reid (DOW-UAP-D183), and additional DIA talking papers and program reviews (DOW-UAP-D184 through DOW-UAP-D188).

Evidence classification

Documented: Contractor report and associated program records. Claims requiring corroboration: Specific investigations and descriptions of unusual material. Not demonstrated: Recovery of nonhuman technology.
