Peer-reviewed accepted manuscript · July 23, 2026

Critical Evaluation of Studies Alleging Evidence for Technosignatures in the POSS1-E Photographic Plates

Applicable science: Photographic artifact assessment, dataset validation and statistical normalization.

Watters and colleagues examine whether historical POSS1-E plate features support reported technosignature interpretations. They report concerns involving plate and scan artifacts, known stars and the statistical treatment of observation opportunities.

Limitations: Findings apply to the datasets examined. The critique does not determine the identity of every plate feature, and VASCO researchers dispute aspects of the analysis.

Scientific preprint · August 31, 2026

Slysh haloes: the waste heat of cold computing as a submillimetre technosignature

Applicable science: Infrared/submillimetre astronomy, thermal emission and natural-source discrimination.

Michael Garrett models hypothetical cold-computing radiators and proposes archival and targeted searches for their far-infrared or submillimetre waste heat.

Natural alternatives: Cold dust, debris, background galaxies, spectral-line sources and instrumental effects can mimic parts of the proposed signature. Candidate assessment must distinguish these possibilities.

Limitations: Detectability depends on hypothetical architecture, temperature, geometry, distance and instrument response. This is a proposed search method; no Slysh halo or extraterrestrial technology detection is reported. Journal acceptance and peer review are not established in the ingested record.
